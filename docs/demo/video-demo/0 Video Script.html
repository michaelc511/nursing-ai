2. New requests arrive Terminal

  • Wake the app and wait for 200:
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code} %{time_total}s\n" https://nurseops-now.fly.dev/api/health
  • Copy the 3 requests into the inbox (this works from any folder):
fly ssh console --app nurseops-now -C "cp /app/data/samples/72148_a_complete.pdf /app/data/samples/73721_b_missing_pt.pdf /app/data/samples/70551_c_not_met.pdf /data/inbox/"
"Three new requests just arrived in the inbox, the way a fax or portal upload would: a lumbar spine MRI, a knee MRI and a brain MRI. In production they land here on their own."

YouTube titles and descriptions

One title and description per clip, plus one for a single full video. Every description ends with the same note that the demo uses synthetic data only.

Full video (all 8 steps)

Title: NurseOps Now: AI Prior Authorization Run by Claude Cowork (Full Demo)

Description:

A full walk-through of NurseOps Now, an AI prototype that helps nurses process prior authorization requests faster. A health plan handles about 15,000 requests a month, waits 4.2 days on average for a decision, and 22% need rework because something is missing. The goal is 40% faster with no added headcount. In this demo: 1. The nurse's queue and success metrics 2. New requests arrive in the inbox 3. Claude Cowork runs the queue through MCP 4. The new cases, with cited AI reviews and an audit trail 5. The nurse approves and pends 6. The medical director denies 7. A leader asks Cowork about operations 8. The nurse asks AI Assist about the policy The rule throughout: AI reads and cites, people decide. The AI never approves or denies. Built with Claude (Anthropic), Claude Cowork, MCP, FastAPI, React and RAG over policy PDFs. Live on Fly.io. Synthetic data only. No real patient information.
