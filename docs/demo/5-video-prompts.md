# NurseOps Now: Video Prompts

For Gemini (Veo) or ChatGPT (Sora). Paste one prompt at a time, then join the clips in an
editor (CapCut, iMovie or Canva). Add the title text "NurseOps Now" and the subtitle
"AI suggests. Nurses decide." in the editor over the white ending of the last clip, because AI video
tools often misspell on-screen text.
